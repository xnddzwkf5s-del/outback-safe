## Cherrybrook Location

Cherrybrook is located in Sydney's Hills District, within Hornsby Shire, approximately 23 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. Coordinates: 33.73°S, 151.05°E.

## Water Sources Ranked by Proximity

### 1. Lane Cove River (~2 km east of Cherrybrook)
- **Direction**: East, downhill
- **Access**: Head east along Purchase Road, cross under the pedestrian tunnel at the M1 (Banana Street tunnel) into Thornleigh. Follow the Lane Cove River walking track from there. The river is accessible via Lane Cove National Park.
- **Walking time**: ~25-35 minutes from central Cherrybrook
- **Water**: Year-round freshwater creek in its upper reaches, tidal in the lower reaches from about Lane Cove township southwards
- **Note**: The river starts near Thornleigh — the upper reaches are a small creek. Larger flows further south towards Lane Cove. Bull sharks present in the lower tidal reaches (below Lane Cove township).
- **Coordinates**: River access near Thornleigh ~-33.763, 151.104

### 2. Berowra Creek (~6 km north-east)
- **Direction**: North-east, down through Berowra Valley
- **Access**: Via Berowra Valley National Park. Take the Great North Walk trail from Hornsby through Berowra Valley. Access points at Crosslands Reserve, Berowra Waters.
- **Walking time**: ~1-1.5 hours
- **Water**: Tidal deepwater creek in its lower reaches. Year-round flow. Upper reaches are more seasonal freshwater.
- **Note**: Part of the Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park / Berowra Valley system. Deep, clean water at Berowra Waters. Can be accessed by boat or kayak from the Hawkesbury. Bushwalking tracks throughout.

### 3. Cowan Creek (~10 km north-east)
- **Direction**: North-east
- **Access**: Via Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park. Access from Cowan train station or via Apple Tree Bay.
- **Walking time**: ~2-2.5 hours
- **Water**: Tidal estuary of the Hawkesbury River. Year-round deepwater.
- **Note**: Bull sharks present. Many beautiful inlets and bays. Popular for boating and kayaking.

### 4. Hawkesbury River (~15 km north)
- **Direction**: North
- **Access**: Via Wisemans Ferry (driving), or Brooklyn (via Pacific Highway/Galston Gorge)
- **Driving time**: ~25 minutes (Wisemans Ferry)
- **Water**: Large tidal river. Year-round water available. Brackish in lower reaches, fresh above Wisemans Ferry.
- **Note**: Bull sharks present in the lower Hawkesbury. Freshwater above Wisemans Ferry. Can be accessed from numerous points. The river is ~300 m wide at Wisemans Ferry. Historic ferry crossing.

### 5. Local Creeks (1-2 km)
- **Pennant Hills Creek**: Flows south from Cherrybrook into the Lane Cove system. Seasonal — may be dry in summer.
- **Devils Creek**: Small tributary in Pennant Hills Park, seasonal.
- **Thyro Creek**: Flows east from Cherrybrook towards the Lane Cove River.

### 6. Swimming Pools & Domestic Water
- **Backyard pools**: Suburban Cherrybrook has many houses with swimming pools
- **Rainwater tanks**: Some houses have rainwater tanks for garden use
- **Tap water**: Provided by Sydney Water from Warragamba Dam (main supply) — treated drinking water

## Directions from Cherrybrook Post Office (Purchase Road)

**To Lane Cove River (closest):**
From Cherrybrook Shopping Centre (41-43 Shepherds Drive):
1. Head east on Shepherds Drive
2. Turn right onto Purchase Road
3. Follow Purchase Road to the pedestrian tunnel under the M1 motorway (Banana Street)
4. Through the tunnel into Thornleigh
5. Follow the Lane Cove River walking track from Thornleigh Oval
6. The river is approximately 800 m from the tunnel

**To Berowra Creek (best natural swimming):**
From Cherrybrook:
1. Head east to Pennant Hills Road
2. Turn left onto Pennant Hills Road north towards Hornsby
3. Take the Pacific Highway north
4. Crosslands Reserve access via Berowra Waters Road
5. Follow Berowra Waters Road to Berowra Waters

**To Hawkesbury River (most reliable year-round):**
From Cherrybrook:
1. Take Castle Hill Road north towards Wisemans Ferry Road
2. Turn left onto Wisemans Ferry Road
3. Follow Wisemans Ferry Road to the river crossing at Wisemans Ferry
4. The Hawkesbury River ferry crosses to the northern side

## Emergency Note

- **000** for life-threatening emergencies
- **Lane Cove National Park**: NPWS Hornsby office available during business hours
- **Water purification**: Always treat natural water before drinking — boil for 3 minutes, use purification tablets, or filter with a 0.2 micron filter. Giardia and Cryptosporidium are present in Australian freshwater.
- **Bushfire season** (Oct-March): Water sources may be affected by ash contamination
- **Do not drink**: Tidal waters (Lane Cove estuary below Lane Cove township, Berowra Creek, Cowan Creek, Hawkesbury lower reaches) are brackish/saline
