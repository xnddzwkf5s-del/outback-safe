# Water Sources — Inland NSW (Canberra, Tablelands, Western Plains)

> Source: OpenStreetMap
> Keywords: Canberra water, Albury water, Wagga Wagga water, Dubbo water, Orange water, Bathurst water, Tamworth water, Armidale water, Broken Hill water, Griffith water, Bourke water, inland NSW water sources, western NSW water sources

## Canberra (ACT)
- **Nearest water**: Lake Burley Griffin (Molonglo River) — runs through the city. Murrumbidgee River (~10 km west).
- **Alternative**: Cotter River (water supply catchment, restricted access), Googong Dam (~15 km south-east)
- **Coordinates**: -35.281, 149.131

## Albury
- **Nearest water**: Murray River — adjacent. Lake Hume (~10 km east).
- **Coordinates**: -36.074, 146.915

## Wodonga (Vic)
- **Nearest water**: Murray River — adjacent (same as Albury).
- **Coordinates**: -36.121, 146.889

## Wagga Wagga
- **Nearest water**: Murrumbidgee River — runs through the city.
- **Coordinates**: -35.108, 147.368

## Deniliquin
- **Nearest water**: Edward River (Murray River anabranch) — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -35.527, 144.960

## Hay
- **Nearest water**: Murrumbidgee River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -34.508, 144.845

## Narrandera
- **Nearest water**: Murrumbidgee River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -34.748, 146.558

## Griffith
- **Nearest water**: Murrumbidgee River (~10 km north). Extensive irrigation channels throughout the area.
- **Coordinates**: -34.287, 146.058

## Leeton
- **Nearest water**: Murrumbidgee River via irrigation channels. Fivebough Swamp (~5 km east, Ramsar wetland).
- **Coordinates**: -34.549, 146.407

## Tumut
- **Nearest water**: Tumut River (Murrumbidgee tributary) — runs through the town. Blowering Dam (~10 km west).
- **Coordinates**: -35.302, 148.222

## Dubbo
- **Nearest water**: Macquarie River — runs through the city. Burrendong Dam (~50 km south-east).
- **Coordinates**: -32.251, 148.608

## Orange
- **Nearest water**: Summer Hill Creek (Macquarie headwaters). Lake Canobolas (~5 km south-west, 30 ha reservoir).
- **Coordinates**: -33.286, 149.102

## Bathurst
- **Nearest water**: Macquarie River — runs through the city.
- **Coordinates**: -33.420, 149.577

## Lithgow
- **Nearest water**: Coxs River (Hawkesbury system). Fish River (Murray-Darling). Lake Lyell (~12 km south, 30 GL reservoir).
- **Features**: The dividing range — water flows east to the Hawkesbury or west to the Murray-Darling depending on location.
- **Coordinates**: -33.482, 150.159

## Mudgee
- **Nearest water**: Cudgegong River (Macquarie tributary) — runs near the town. Windamere Dam (~20 km south-west).
- **Coordinates**: -32.592, 149.588

## Cowra
- **Nearest water**: Lachlan River — runs through the town. Wyangala Dam (~40 km south-east).
- **Coordinates**: -33.835, 148.689

## Forbes
- **Nearest water**: Lachlan River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -33.385, 148.008

## Parkes

## Condobolin

## Hillston

## Wellington

- **Coordinates**: -32.559, 148.945

## Tamworth

## Gunnedah

## Narrabri
- **Nearest water**: Namoi River — runs through the town.

## Walgett
- **Nearest water**: Barwon River — runs through the town (junction of Namoi and Barwon).
- **Coordinates**: -30.022, 148.116

## Brewarrina
- **Nearest water**: Barwon River — runs through the town. Famous Aboriginal fish traps (Ngunnhu).
- **Coordinates**: -29.961, 146.861

## Bourke
- **Nearest water**: Darling River — runs through the town. Often dry upstream.
- **Coordinates**: -30.091, 145.936

## Nyngan
- **Nearest water**: Bogan River (Darling tributary) — runs near the town. Intermittent.
- **Coordinates**: -31.565, 147.200

## Warren
- **Nearest water**: Macquarie River — runs through the town. Macquarie Marshes (~30 km north-west).
- **Coordinates**: -31.701, 147.835

## Cobar
- **Nearest water**: Nyngan/Bogan system (~100 km east). None locally — relies on groundwater and tank water.
- **Coordinates**: -31.498, 145.834

## Wilcannia
- **Nearest water**: Darling River — runs through the town. Often dry during drought.
- **Coordinates**: -31.559, 143.378

## Menindee
- **Nearest water**: Darling River, Menindee Lakes — adjacent. Chain of lakes provide water storage.
- **Coordinates**: -32.394, 142.418

## Broken Hill
- **Nearest water**: Darling River at Menindee (~110 km south-east). Menindee Lakes (~110 km). Stepnicks Creek (small local supply, limited).
- **Note**: Broken Hill has the only permanently flowing water source in far western NSW — the Darling River system. The town relies on pipelines from the Murray-Darling. Estimated 4-5 year supply in severe drought.
- **Coordinates**: -31.956, 141.465

## Wentworth
- **Nearest water**: Junction of the Murray and Darling rivers — adjacent. Year-round water.
- **Coordinates**: -34.110, 141.917

## Armidale
- **Nearest water**: Dumaresq Creek (Macquarie/Murray-Darling headwaters — flows west). Malpas Dam (~10 km south, 15 GL reservoir).
- **Coordinates**: -30.514, 151.670
