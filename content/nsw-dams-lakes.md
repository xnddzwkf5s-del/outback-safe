## Sydney Catchment Dams (WaterNSW)

These dams supply Sydney's drinking water (~4.5 million people):

### Warragamba Dam
- **River**: Warragamba River (tributary of Hawkesbury/Nepean system)
- **Capacity**: 2,031 GL — Sydney's largest water storage
- **Location**: ~60 km west of Sydney near Penrith
- **Completed**: 1960
- **Supplies**: ~80% of Sydney's water
- **Catchment**: 9,051 km² (Blue Mountains, Southern Highlands)
- **Lake**: Lake Burragorang
- **Spillway**: One of the world's largest ungated spillways

### Nepean Dam
- **River**: Nepean River
- **Capacity**: 67 GL
- **Location**: Near Bargo, southern Sydney
- **Completed**: 1935

### Avon Dam
- **River**: Avon River
- **Capacity**: 214 GL
- **Location**: Near Robertson, Southern Highlands
- **Completed**: 1928

### Cataract Dam
- **River**: Cataract River
- **Capacity**: 94 GL
- **Location**: Near Appin
- **Completed**: 1907 — oldest major Sydney water supply dam

### Cordeaux Dam
- **River**: Cordeaux River
- **Capacity**: 93 GL
- **Location**: Near Robertson
- **Completed**: 1926

### Woronora Dam
- **River**: Woronora River
- **Capacity**: 71 GL
- **Location**: Near Engadine, southern Sydney
- **Completed**: 1941

### Tallowa Dam
- **River**: Shoalhaven River
- **Capacity**: 85 GL
- **Location**: Near Kangaroo Valley
- **Completed**: 1977
- **Purpose**: Pumps water into the Nepean system (Shoalhaven Transfers Scheme)

### Prospect Reservoir
- **River**: Off-river storage (Warragamba supply)
- **Capacity**: 50 GL
- **Location**: Western Sydney
- **Completed**: 1888 (enlarged multiple times)
- **Purpose**: Terminal storage for Sydney's water supply

## Major Murray-Darling Basin Dams

### Hume Dam
- **River**: Murray River
- **Capacity**: 3,005 GL — largest dam in NSW by volume
- **Location**: Albury-Wodonga
- **Completed**: 1936 (enlarged)
- **Lake**: Lake Hume
- **Purpose**: Irrigation, flood mitigation, hydro-power (50 MW)

### Dartmouth Dam
- **River**: Mitta Mitta River (Murray headwaters)
- **Capacity**: 4,000 GL — largest in the Murray-Darling Basin
- **Location**: Near Dartmouth, Victoria (regulates Murray for NSW)
- **Completed**: 1979
- **Purpose**: Irrigation security, hydro-power (150 MW)

### Burrinjuck Dam
- **River**: Murrumbidgee River
- **Capacity**: 1,026 GL
- **Location**: Near Yass
- **Completed**: 1928
- **Lake**: Lake Burrinjuck
- **Purpose**: Irrigation, hydro-power

### Burrendong Dam
- **River**: Macquarie River
- **Capacity**: 1,190 GL
- **Location**: Near Wellington
- **Completed**: 1967
- **Lake**: Lake Burrendong
- **Purpose**: Irrigation, flood mitigation, Macquarie Marshes environmental flows

### Wyangala Dam
- **River**: Lachlan River
- **Capacity**: 1,220 GL
- **Location**: Near Cowra
- **Completed**: 1935 (enlarged)
- **Lake**: Lake Wyangala
- **Purpose**: Irrigation, flood mitigation

### Keepit Dam

### Copeton Dam

### Split Rock Dam

### Glenbawn Dam

### Chichester Dam

### Grahamstown Dam

### Lake Liddell

## Natural Lakes and Wetlands

### Lake George
- **Location**: Southern Tablelands, near Canberra
- **Area**: ~80-150 km² (variable)
- **Features**: Endorheic lake (no outlet). Can be completely dry. Intermittently filled. Deepest recorded: 7.4 m. A 25 km long lake on the Lake George Fault line. Occasionally floods then dries for years.

### Lake Burley Griffin
- **Location**: Canberra (artificial)
- **Area**: 6.64 km²
- **Constructed**: 1964
- **River**: Molonglo River (Murrumbidgee tributary)
- **Depth**: Average 4 m, maximum 18 m

### Macquarie Marshes
- **Location**: Near Warren, central-western NSW
- **Area**: ~200,000 ha
- **River**: Macquarie River
- **Status**: Ramsar-listed. Internationally significant waterbird habitat
- **Features**: One of Australia's largest semi-permanent wetlands. Supports breeding colonies of ibis, egrets, herons.
- **Water source**: Macquarie River flows (regulated by Burrendong Dam)

### Menindee Lakes
- **Location**: Near Menindee, far western NSW
- **River**: Darling River
- **Area**: ~47,000 ha when full
- **Features**: Chain of natural ephemeral lakes modified for water storage. Includes Lakes Menindee, Pamamaroo, Wetherell, Cawndilla. Key waterbird habitat. Site of mass fish kills during drought (2018-19, 2023). Important for Darling River flow regulation.

### Gwydir Wetlands
- **Location**: Near Moree
- **Area**: ~100,000 ha
- **River**: Gwydir River
- **Features**: Ramsar-listed. Important waterbird habitat. Fed by Gwydir River regulated by Copeton Dam.

### Fivebough Swamp
- **Location**: Near Leeton
- **Area**: 516 ha
- **Features**: Ramsar-listed wetland. Important for migratory wading birds. Managed for bird habitat.

## Coastal Lakes and Lagoons

### Lake Macquarie
- **Location**: Central Coast, near Newcastle
- **Area**: 110 km² — largest coastal saltwater lake in NSW
- **Connection**: Tasman Sea at Swansea (dredged channel)
- **Features**: Saltwater tidal lake. Bull sharks present. Popular for sailing and fishing.
- **Main inflow**: Dora Creek

### Tuggerah Lake
- **Location**: Central Coast
- **Area**: 54 km²
- **Connection**: Tasman Sea at The Entrance (permanently open)
- **Features**: Coastal lagoon. Wyong River is main inflow. Popular for boating and fishing.

### Myall Lakes
- **Location**: Great Lakes region, north of Port Stephens
- **Area**: ~20,000 ha
- **Features**: One of the largest coastal lake systems in NSW. Ramsar-listed. The Myall River connects to the coast. Popular for water sports and fishing.

### St Georges Basin
- **Location**: South coast, near Sussex Inlet
- **Area**: 40 km²
- **Features**: Coastal lagoon system, connected to the Tasman Sea via Sussex Inlet.

### Lake Conjola
- **Location**: South coast, near Milton
- **Area**: 7 km²
- **Features**: Popular holiday and fishing destination.

### Durras Lake
- **Location**: South of Batemans Bay
- **Features**: Coastal lake within Murramarang National Park.
