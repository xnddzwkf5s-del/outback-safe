# Northern NSW Coastal Rivers — Tweed River to Manning River

> Source: Wikipedia, NSW Office of Water
> Keywords: Tweed River, Richmond River, Clarence River, Bellinger River, Macleay River, Hastings River, Manning River, Northern Rivers NSW, north coast rivers, NSW coastal rivers

## Tweed River
- **Length**: 78 km
- **Source**: Border Ranges, McPherson Range
- **Mouth**: Coral Sea at Tweed Heads
- **Key tributaries**: Oxley River, Rous River
- **Key towns**: Murwillumbah, Tweed Heads
- **Features**: Short, steep coastal river. The Tweed Valley is fertile, known for sugar cane and bananas. Flows through Caldera rim of an ancient volcanic shield (Mt Warning caldera). Can flood rapidly.
- **Estuary**: Bar of the Tweed River needs regular dredging

## Brunswick River
- **Length**: 25 km
- **Source**: Byron Bay hinterland
- **Mouth**: Coral Sea at Brunswick Heads
- **Key towns**: Mullumbimby, Brunswick Heads
- **Features**: Small coastal river, popular for camping and canoeing

## Richmond River
- **Length**: 245 km
- **Source**: McPherson Range, Lamington Plateau
- **Mouth**: Coral Sea at Ballina
- **Key tributaries**: Wilsons River (passes through Lismore), Leycester Creek, Coopers Creek
- **Key towns**: Lismore, Casino, Coraki, Ballina, Kyogle
- **Features**: Major Northern Rivers system. Known for extreme flooding (2017, 2022 Lismore floods). Extensive floodplain agriculture (sugar, beef, dairy). Ballina's coastal breakwall is a popular fishing spot.

## Clarence River
- **Length**: 394 km — largest coastal river system in NSW by discharge
- **Source**: McPherson Range, Border Ranges
- **Mouth**: Coral Sea at Yamba/Iluka
- **Key tributaries**: Mann River, Nymboida River, Orara River, Timbarra River, Coldstream River
- **Key towns**: Grafton, Maclean, Yamba, Iluka, Ulmarra, Copmanhurst
- **Features**: Largest coastal river in NSW by water volume. Oldest navigable river in Australia (used by paddle steamers). Extensive wetlands. Famous for the Jacaranda trees in Grafton. Forms the Clarence Valley. Good fishing (Murray cod, bass, bream).

## Bellinger River
- **Length**: 60 km
- **Source**: New England tablelands
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Urunga
- **Key tributaries**: Never Never River, Rosewood River, Kalang River
- **Key towns**: Bellingen, Urunga
- **Features**: Pristine river with impressive waterfalls. Rainforest-lined banks. The Never Never River is a notable sub-catchment. Known for platypus population.

## Nambucca River
- **Length**: 80 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Nambucca Heads
- **Key tributaries**: Taylors Arm
- **Key towns**: Macksville, Nambucca Heads
- **Features**: Popular holiday destination, oyster farming in the estuary. The V-Wall at Nambucca Heads is famous for painted rocks.

## Macleay River
- **Length**: 298 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Armidale
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at South West Rocks
- **Key tributaries**: Apsley River, Chandler River, Styx River, Gara River, Wollomombi River
- **Key towns**: Kempsey, Bellbrook, South West Rocks
- **Features**: Wollomombi Falls (one of Australia's highest waterfalls at 220 m). Trial Bay Gaol at South West Rocks. Oyster and fishing industry. Trial Bay is popular for prawning.

## Hastings River
- **Length**: 170 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Walcha
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Port Macquarie
- **Key tributaries**: Forbes River, Ellenborough River, Maria River, Wilson River
- **Key towns**: Port Macquarie, Wauchope
- **Features**: The Hastings is one of NSW's most popular holiday rivers. Port Macquarie has excellent estuarine fishing. Ellenborough Falls (200 m drop, one of the highest in the Southern Hemisphere).

## Manning River
- **Length**: 240 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range, Barrington Tops
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea near Harrington, Old Bar
- **Key tributaries**: Barrington River, Gloucester River, Nowendoc River, Barnard River, Pigna Barney River
- **Key towns**: Taree, Wingham, Gloucester
- **Features**: One of the major coastal systems. Barrington Tops provides consistent year-round flow. Wingham Brush is a rare surviving lowland rainforest remnant. The Manning has a split-mouth system entering the sea at two points.
