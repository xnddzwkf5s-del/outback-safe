# Water Sources — Sydney & Surrounds

> Source: WaterNSW, OpenStreetMap
> Keywords: Sydney water sources, nearest water Sydney CBD, Parramatta water, Penrith water, Campbelltown water, Liverpool water, Hornsby water, Sutherland water, Bankstown water, Blacktown water, Hills District water, Ryde water

## Sydney CBD
- **Nearest natural water**: Sydney Harbour (Parramatta River / Port Jackson) — adjacent to the city. Saltwater tidal estuary.
- **Nearest freshwater**: Lane Cove River (~7 km north-west, freshwater above Lane Cove township)
- **Nearest swimming**: Bondi Beach, Coogee (ocean, ~7 km east)
- **Water supply**: Warragamba Dam via Sydney Water system

## Parramatta
- **Nearest water**: Parramatta River — runs through the city. Tidal saltwater/brackish. Bull sharks present.
- **Nearest freshwater**: Lake Parramatta (~2 km north) — freshwater reservoir, popular for swimming.
- **Coordinates**: -33.812, 151.003

## Penrith
- **Nearest water**: Nepean River — runs through the city. Year-round freshwater flow.
- **Access**: Tench Reserve, Nepean River walking track
- **Note**: Warragamba Dam is 5 km south, Lake Burragorang is accessible but restricted
- **Coordinates**: -33.751, 150.694

## Campbelltown
- **Nearest water**: Georges River (Kentlyn area, ~6 km east). Upper Georges River is freshwater
- **Alternative**: Nepean River (~15 km west)
- **Coordinates**: -34.065, 150.817

## Liverpool
- **Nearest water**: Georges River — runs through Liverpool. Tidal, brackish in lower reaches. Bull sharks present.
- **Freshwater**: Upstream towards Campbelltown
- **Coordinates**: -33.921, 150.925

## Hornsby
- **Nearest water**: Berowra Creek (~4 km north-east, via Berowra Valley National Park). Tidal deepwater.
- **Alternative**: Lane Cove River (~5 km south)
- **Coordinates**: -33.704, 151.100

## Sutherland / Cronulla
- **Nearest water**: Woronora River (~5 km west). Hacking River (Royal National Park, ~3 km south).
- **Ocean**: Cronulla beaches (adjacent)
- **Coordinates**: -34.030, 151.057

## Bankstown
- **Nearest water**: Georges River (~4 km west, tidal)
- **Bankstown Water**: Tap water from Warragamba system

## Blacktown
- **Nearest water**: Eastern Creek (~3 km east), Nepean River (~10 km west)
- **Coordinates**: -33.771, 150.908

## Ryde
- **Nearest water**: Parramatta River / Lane Cove River (adjacent south-west). Tidal saltwater.
- **Coordinates**: -33.813, 151.102

## Central Coast (Gosford)
- **Nearest water**: Brisbane Water (tidal estuary) — adjacent to Gosford
- **Alternative**: Tuggerah Lake (~5 km north), Wyong River
- **Ocean**: Terrigal Beach, Avoca Beach (~5 km east)
- **Coordinates**: -33.427, 151.342

## Blue Mountains (Katoomba)
- **Nearest water**: Kedumba River (~5 km east, down the escarpment). Coxs River (~8 km west near Lithgow).
- **Alternative**: Leura Cascades, Gordon Creek, various waterfalls
- **Note**: Most creeks in the Blue Mountains are seasonal. Water is limited on the plateau in dry periods.
- **Coordinates**: -33.713, 150.312

## Southern Highlands (Bowral/Mittagong)
- **Nearest water**: Wingecarribee River (~3 km south), Nepean River (~8 km east)
- **Alternative**: Lake Fitzroy (Fitzroy Falls, ~15 km south-east)
- **Coordinates**: -34.479, 150.418
