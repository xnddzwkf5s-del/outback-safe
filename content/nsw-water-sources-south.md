# Water Sources — Southern NSW (Wollongong to Victoria Border)

> Source: OpenStreetMap
> Keywords: Wollongong water, Nowra water, Batemans Bay water, Ulladulla water, Moruya water, Narooma water, Merimbula water, Eden water, Bega water, Cooma water, Jindabyne water, south coast water sources

## Wollongong
- **Nearest water**: Pacific Ocean — adjacent. Lake Illawarra (~5 km south, saltwater lagoon).
- **Freshwater**: Cordeaux River (~10 km north-west, Avon Dam area). Cataract River (~15 km north).
- **Coordinates**: -34.423, 150.891

## Nowra / Bomaderry
- **Nearest water**: Shoalhaven River — runs through the town. Tidal below Nowra bridge. Bull sharks present.
- **Coordinates**: -34.891, 150.600

## Ulladulla
- **Nearest water**: Pacific Ocean (adjacent). Lake Burrill (~5 km south).
- **Coordinates**: -35.361, 150.472

## Batemans Bay
- **Nearest water**: Clyde River estuary — adjacent to the town. Pacific Ocean adjacent.
- **Alternative**: Buckenbowra River (~5 km west).
- **Coordinates**: -35.708, 150.175

## Moruya
- **Nearest water**: Moruya River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -35.912, 150.081

## Narooma
- **Nearest water**: Wagonga Inlet (estuary) — adjacent. Pacific Ocean adjacent.
- **Coordinates**: -36.216, 150.133

## Merimbula
- **Nearest water**: Merimbula Lake (coastal lagoon) — adjacent. Pacific Ocean adjacent.
- **Coordinates**: -36.889, 149.917

## Bega
- **Nearest water**: Bega River — runs through the town. Seasonal flow.
- **Coordinates**: -36.678, 149.842

## Eden
- **Nearest water**: Twofold Bay (Caluwet Bay, Quarantine Bay) — adjacent, ocean bay. Towamba River (~10 km south at Wonboyn).
- **Coordinates**: -37.066, 149.909

## Cooma
- **Nearest water**: Cooma Back Creek, Murrumbidgee River (~15 km west). Eucumbene River (~30 km south).
- **Alternative**: Lake Eucumbene (~30 km south, Snowy Scheme), Snowy River (~25 km east)
- **Coordinates**: -36.234, 149.127

## Jindabyne
- **Nearest water**: Lake Jindabyne (Snowy River) — adjacent to the town. Year-round available.
- **Alternative**: Thredbo River, Crackenback River (both upstream)
- **Coordinates**: -36.425, 148.619

## Bombala
- **Nearest water**: Bombala River (Snowy River tributary). Snowy River proper (~10 km south).
- **Coordinates**: -36.912, 149.237

## Goulburn
- **Nearest water**: Wollondilly River — runs through the town (Lachlan/Murray-Darling headwaters).
- **Coordinates**: -34.752, 149.718

## Young
- **Nearest water**: Burrangong Creek (Lachlan tributary). Lake Burrendong area (~60 km north-west).
- **Coordinates**: -34.313, 148.303

## Yass
- **Nearest water**: Yass River (Murrumbidgee tributary). Burrinjuck Dam (~20 km south-west, Murrumbidgee).
- **Coordinates**: -34.844, 148.912

## Queanbeyan
- **Nearest water**: Queanbeyan River (Molonglo tributary). Molonglo River ~2 km north. Lake Burley Griffin ~10 km west.
- **Coordinates**: -35.351, 149.234
