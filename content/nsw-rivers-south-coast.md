# Southern NSW Coastal Rivers — Shoalhaven River to Snowy River

> Source: Wikipedia, NSW Office of Water
> Keywords: Shoalhaven River, Clyde River, Moruya River, Tuross River, Bega River, Snowy River, south coast rivers NSW, southern NSW rivers

## Shoalhaven River
- **Length**: 327 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Cooma
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Shoalhaven Heads
- **Key tributaries**: Kangaroo River, Mongarlowe River, Corang River, Ettrema Creek, Yarrunga Creek
- **Key towns**: Nowra, Bomaderry, Braidwood
- **Features**: The Shoalhaven is one of the largest coastal rivers in NSW south of Sydney. The Shoalhaven Scheme includes **Tallowa Dam**, which pumps water into the Nepean system for Sydney's water supply. The lower river forms an extensive floodplain. **Fitzroy Falls** and the **Belmore Falls** are on tributaries. Canoeing and kayaking popular. The river has bull sharks in the lower tidal reaches.

## Clyde River
- **Length**: 102 km
- **Source**: Budawang Range
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Batemans Bay
- **Key tributaries**: Yadboro River, Bimberamala River, Buckenbowra River
- **Key towns**: Batemans Bay, Nelligen
- **Features**: One of NSW's least developed major rivers. The lower estuary is known for oyster farming. Flows through Clyde River National Park and Budawang National Park. Pristine wilderness in the upper reaches. Good fishing (bream, flathead, whiting).

## Moruya River
- **Length**: 70 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Mt Wadbilliga
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Moruya Heads
- **Key tributaries**: Deua River, Araluen Creek
- **Key towns**: Moruya, Bodalla
- **Features**: The Deua River is the main tributary, flowing through Deua National Park. Historic gold mining area (Araluen goldfields). Oyster leases in the estuary. Granite quarries near Moruya were used to build the Sydney Harbour Bridge.

## Tuross River
- **Length**: 80 km
- **Source**: Badja State Forest
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Tuross Head
- **Key towns**: Eurobodalla, Tuross Head
- **Features**: Large shallow estuary system. Known for excellent fishing and birdwatching. **Tuross Lake** and **Coral Sea** connect near the mouth. Popular with kayakers.

## Bega River
- **Length**: 70 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Nimmitabel
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Tathra
- **Key tributaries**: Brogo River, Wolumla Creek
- **Key towns**: Bega, Tathra
- **Features**: The Bega Valley is known for dairy farming (Bega Cheese). The river is relatively small but supports a significant coastal floodplain. Brogo River Dam provides irrigation.

## Towamba River
- **Length**: 75 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Bombala
- **Mouth**: Tasman Sea at Wonboyn
- **Key towns**: Eden (nearby)
- **Features**: Southernmost coastal river in NSW. Remote, forested catchment. **Lake Wonboyn** at the mouth is a popular barramundi fishing location (stocked).

## Snowy River
- **Length**: 352 km (only ~100 km in NSW, the rest in Victoria)
- **Source**: Snowy Mountains, Mt Kosciuszko area
- **Mouth**: Bass Strait in Victoria (Marlo)
- **Key tributaries** (NSW): Eucumbene River, Thredbo River, Crackenback River, Mowamba River, Gungarlin River
- **Key towns** (NSW): Jindabyne, Thredbo, Perisher Valley
- **Features**: The Snowy River was dramatically altered by the Snowy Mountains Scheme (1950s-1970s). Most of its flow is diverted through tunnels to the Murray and Murrumbidgee systems for irrigation. From a natural flow of ~2,500 GL/year at Jindabyne, it was reduced to as little as 1% in the 1980s. Environmental flows now release ~212 GL/year (28%). The Snowy River is famous for its alpine scenery, whitewater rafting, and the Snowy River National Park (Victoria side).
- **Snowy Mountains Scheme**: 16 major dams, 7 power stations, 225 km of tunnels. Transfers water from the Snowy River catchment to the Murray and Murrumbidgee for irrigation.
