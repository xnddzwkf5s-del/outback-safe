# South Australia — Nearest Water Sources for Major Towns

> **Last updated:** 2026-07-28
> **Purpose:** Quick-reference for nearest accessible water body to every major SA settlement.
> **Key:** 🟢 potable (treatable) | 🟡 treat with care | 🔴 saline | ⚠️ ephemeral/unreliable | **Bold** = primary water supply

---

## Adelaide Metropolitan Area

### Adelaide (CBD)
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Torrens River (Torrens Lake)** — within city, runs through CBD
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Kangaroo Creek Reservoir / Hope Valley Reservoir** — ~12 km east (metro water supply)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Onkaparinga River (upper)** via Mount Bold pipeline — 30 km south
- **Coordinates:** -34.9285, 138.6007

---

## Adelaide Hills

### Hahndorf
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Onkaparinga River** — ~2 km east (close to headwaters near Verdun)
- **Better option:** 🟢 Mount Bold Reservoir — ~15 km south-west
- **Alternative:** Rainwater tanks common throughout the Adelaide Hills
- **Coordinates:** -35.0299, 138.8082

### Lobethal
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Onkaparinga River (upper)** — ~3 km south-west, near Charleston
- **Alternative:** Local creeks feed into the Onkaparinga system; rainwater tanks
- **Coordinates:** -34.9051, 138.8746

### Mount Barker
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Western Flat Creek / Mount Barker Creek** — within town, feeds into Bremer River system
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Mount Bold Reservoir** — ~20 km west
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Murray River** — ~50 km east (via Murray Bridge)
- **Notes:** Fastest-growing Adelaide Hills town; significant rainwater tank usage; town water is piped from the Murray (Mannum–Adelaide pipeline)
- **Coordinates:** -35.0605, 138.8584

## Barossa Valley

### Tanunda
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **North Para River** — ~2 km north of town centre
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **South Para Reservoir** — ~20 km south (near Williamstown)
- **Notes:** North Para River drains the Barossa; intermittent summer flow. Barossa Council water from the Murray via pipeline.
- **Coordinates:** -34.5237, 138.9596

### Gawler
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Gawler River (North Para + South Para confluence)** — runs through town
- **Better option:** 🟢 **South Para Reservoir** — ~15 km south-east
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Murray River** — ~70 km east (pipeline)
- **Notes:** Confluence town; both branches of the Para are seasonal. Town water from Murray pipeline.
- **Coordinates:** -34.5991, 138.7451

## Mid-North

### Kapunda
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Light River** — runs through/adjacent to town (eastern edge)
- **Caution:** Historic copper mine drainage — test water for acidity/metals
- **Coordinates:** -34.3387, 138.9161

### Clare
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Hutt River / Bungaree Creek** — runs through town (tributary of Wakefield River)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Clare Valley reservoirs / bores** — town water supply
- **Notes:** Clare Valley wine region; multiple small creeks and dams in the district. Town water from local catchment.
- **Coordinates:** -33.8326, 138.6103

### Burra
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Burra Creek** — runs through town (tributary of the River Darling/Murray system via the Broughton)
- **Alternative:** Bores and local reservoirs
- **Notes:** Historic copper mining town; Burra Creek is seasonal. Town water from local bores and pipeline.
- **Coordinates:** -33.6715, 138.9283

## Riverland (Murray River)

### Renmark
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; river runs along northern edge
- **Notes:** First major SA town on the Murray; irrigation hub. Excellent river access. Lock 5 nearby.
- **Coordinates:** -34.1740, 140.7483

### Berri
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; river runs along northern edge
- **Notes:** Major citrus-growing centre; Lock 4 within town. Multiple boat ramps.
- **Coordinates:** -34.2875, 140.6014

### Loxton
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; river runs along western edge
- **Notes:** Irrigation district; river accessible at multiple points. Lock 3 nearby.
- **Coordinates:** -34.4504, 140.5694

### Waikerie
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; river to the north
- **Notes:** Major citrus and stone fruit growing; Lock 2 nearby. Ferry crossing to the north side.
- **Coordinates:** -34.1814, 139.9856

### Morgan
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; historic river port at the great bend
- **Notes:** Key historical town — Morgan was once SA's busiest river port. The Murray turns south here. Lock 2 nearby. Morgan–Whyalla pipeline starts here.
- **Coordinates:** -34.0343, 139.6675

## Murraylands

### Murray Bridge
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Murray River** — riverbank town; river runs through the centre
- **Notes:** Largest town on the lower Murray; major road and rail bridge crossing. Key regional centre with multiple boat ramps, wharves, and riverfront access.
- **Coordinates:** -35.1199, 139.2744

## Lower Lakes & Coorong

### Goolwa
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Lake Alexandrina / Murray Mouth** — town sits directly on the lake and the Murray's final reach
- **Alternative (saline):** 🔴 **The Coorong** — starts just east at the Murray Mouth
- **Notes:** Historic river port; Goolwa Barrages control the lake-sea interface. The Murray Mouth is ~5 km east. Boat access to the entire lower Murray.
- **Coordinates:** -35.5010, 138.7801

### Strathalbyn
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Angas River** — runs through town
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Lake Alexandrina** — ~15 km east (via Milang)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Murray River (lower reaches)** — ~25 km east
- **Notes:** Angas River is intermittent; flows seasonally. Strathalbyn's town water is piped from the Murray (via Clayton treatment plant).
- **Coordinates:** -35.2608, 138.8917

## Fleurieu Peninsula

### Victor Harbor
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Inman River** — runs through town to Encounter Bay
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Hindmarsh River** — runs through town (west side)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Lake Alexandrina / Murray River** — ~35 km north-east (pipeline connected)
- **Notes:** Coastal town; Inman and Hindmarsh Rivers are seasonal. Town water from the Murray via pipeline. Encounter Bay is saltwater.
- **Coordinates:** -35.5510, 138.6225

## South East

### Mount Gambier
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Blue Lake / Valley Lake** — within town (crater lakes in an extinct volcano)
- **Notes:** Blue Lake is Mount Gambier's primary water supply — one of SA's most reliable natural freshwater sources. Valley Lake and Leg of Mutton Lake also in the crater complex. Groundwater from the Gambier Limestone aquifer is excellent quality. The Blue Lake famously turns vivid blue each November–March.
- **Coordinates:** -37.8293, 140.7820

### Naracoorte
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Naracoorte Creek** — runs through town (tributary of the Mosquito Creek / Glenelg River system)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Groundwater bores** — town water from the Gambier Basin aquifer
- **Notes:** Limestone Coast; groundwater is the primary supply. Naracoorte Caves National Park nearby — cave water pools but accessibility limited.
- **Coordinates:** -36.9569, 140.7413

---

## Eyre Peninsula

### Whyalla
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Morgan–Whyalla Pipeline** — town water (piped from the Murray River, ~350 km away)
- **Nearest natural water:** 🔴 **Spencer Gulf** — saltwater; adjacent to town
- **Alternative:** ⚠️ **Iron Knob area creeks** — ephemeral, ~50 km west; not reliable
- **Notes:** Whyalla has no natural freshwater source. The Morgan–Whyalla pipeline (opened 1944) is its lifeline. Without it, Whyalla is dependent on desalination or imported water. Small local catchment dams exist but are unreliable.
- **Coordinates:** -33.0353, 137.5858

### Port Lincoln
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Proper Bay / Boston Bay creeks** — seasonal creeks feeding into the bays
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Tod Reservoir / groundwater bores** — town water supply
- **Notes:** Eyre Peninsula water is primarily from the Uley South and Lincoln Basin groundwater lenses (freshwater aquifers overlying saline groundwater). Tod Reservoir was historically the main supply but has been decommissioned. Town now relies on bore fields and a desalination plant.
- **Coordinates:** -34.7226, 135.8621

### Ceduna
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Murat Bay creeks / local catchment** — seasonal
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Groundwater bores** — town water from local aquifer
- **Notes:** Arid western Eyre Peninsula; town water from bores in the Ceduna Basin. Desalination also supplements supply. No permanent rivers within ~200 km.
- **Coordinates:** -32.1257, 133.6749

### Streaky Bay
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Local catchment / groundwater bores** — town water supply
- **Nearest natural water:** 🔴 **Streaky Bay (Spencer Gulf)** — saltwater
- **Notes:** Coastal town; no permanent freshwater rivers. Relies on bores and a small desalination plant. Local creeks flow only after rain.
- **Coordinates:** -32.7979, 134.2114

### Wudinna
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Wudinna catchment dams / granite rock catchments** — town water
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Tod Reservoir pipeline** — connected to Port Lincoln system
- **Notes:** Central Eyre Peninsula; extremely dry. The Wudinna district pioneered granite outcrop water harvesting — runoff from granite inselbergs (e.g., Polda Rock, Mount Wudinna) is captured in dams. Groundwater is highly saline across most of the area.
- **Coordinates:** -33.0480, 135.4642

### Kimba
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Kimba catchment dams / groundwater bores** — town water
- **Nearest lake:** 🔴 **Lake Gairdner** — ~150 km north-west (salt pan — no potable water)
- **Alternative:** ⚠️ **Local bores** — variable quality
- **Notes:** Half-way across Australia marker town; on Goyder's Line — the boundary of viable rainfall agriculture. Water supply from local bores and rainwater catchment. Lake Gilles and other ephemeral salt lakes nearby but undrinkable.
- **Coordinates:** -33.1418, 136.4186

## Spencer Gulf

### Port Pirie
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Port Pirie River / Crystal Brook** — ~10 km south
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Napperby Reservoir / Murray pipeline** — town water via Morgan–Whyalla pipeline branch
- **Nearest large water:** 🔴 **Spencer Gulf** — adjacent; saltwater
- **Notes:** Industrial port city. Town water from Murray pipeline. The Port Pirie River is small, seasonal, and historically contaminated by smelter operations — do not drink untreated from the river.
- **Coordinates:** -33.1764, 138.0105

### Port Augusta
- **Nearest water**: 🔴 **Spencer Gulf (head of the gulf)** — saltwater; town sits at the apex
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Morgan–Whyalla Pipeline** — town water from the Murray
- **Nearest freshwater:** ⚠️ **Flinders Ranges creeks (e.g., Pichi Richi area)** — ~30 km north-east; seasonal
- **Notes:** The "Crossroads of Australia" — where Highway 1 meets the Stuart Highway. No natural freshwater river. Wholly dependent on the Morgan–Whyalla pipeline. Local creeks from the Flinders (dry most of the year) are unreliable.
- **Coordinates:** -32.4922, 137.7624

## Far North / Outback

### Roxby Downs
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Great Artesian Basin bores** — town water supply
- **Nearest natural surface water:** ⚠️ **Lake Torrens** — ~50 km east (salt pan, no potable water)
- **Notes:** Purpose-built mining town (Olympic Dam copper-uranium mine). No natural surface water. Town is supplied from bore fields tapping the Great Artesian Basin (GAB). GAB water is warm (~40–60°C emerging), slightly mineralised but potable, and accessible via free-flowing bores.
- **Coordinates:** -30.5532, 136.8953

### Coober Pedy
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Great Artesian Basin bores** — town water via pipeline from bore fields
- **Nearest natural surface water:** ⚠️ None within ~100 km — true desert
- **Notes:** Famous opal mining town; entirely waterless at surface. Water is piped from bore fields tapping the GAB ~25 km north-east. The water is slightly saline and mineralised but treated for town supply. Local knowledge of bore locations is essential.
- **Coordinates:** -29.0138, 134.7549

### Marree
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Frome River (Lake Eyre drainage)** — ~30 km west; ephemeral
- **Better option:** ⚠️ **Great Artesian Basin bore** — at Marree; warm mineralised water
- **Nearest lake:** 🔴 **Lake Eyre South** — ~60 km north-west
- **Notes:** Historic railhead (Old Ghan railway); junction of the Oodnadatta Track and Birdsville Track. GAB bore provides town water. Lake Eyre South is saline. Frome River waterholes are unreliable.
- **Coordinates:** -29.6461, 138.0649

### Oodnadatta
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Great Artesian Basin bore** — at town (bore-fed waterhole / tank)
- **Nearest natural water:** ⚠️ **Neales River catchment** — seasonal; infrequent flow
- **Notes:** Remote outback town on the Oodnadatta Track. No natural surface water. The GAB bore is the town's lifeline; the waterhole it feeds is an oasis in the desert. Neales River (Peake) flows only after extreme rainfall events.
- **Coordinates:** -27.5490, 135.4472

### Innamincka
- **Nearest water**: 🟢 **Cooper Creek** — town sits directly on Cooper Creek
- **Notes:** One of the few outback towns with a natural permanent water source. Cooper Creek waterholes (including Cullyamurra Waterhole, one of Australia's largest permanent waterholes) are the town's supply. In drought, waterholes shrink and become saline. Town bore provides backup.
- **Coordinates:** -27.7088, 140.7371

### Moomba (Gas Fields)
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Cooper Creek** — ~100 km north (near Innamincka)
- **On-site:** 🟢 **Bore water (Great Artesian Basin)** — industrial camp supply
- **Notes:** Remote Santos gas processing facility. No natural surface water. Camp water from GAB bores. Cooper Creek is the nearest natural surface water but is distant and accessible only by 4WD on unsealed roads/tracks. Self-sufficiency is essential in this area.
- **Coordinates:** -28.1095, 140.2055

## Flinders Ranges

### Quorn
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Pichi Richi Creek** — runs through town (ephemeral)
- **Better option:** 🟡 **Quorn Reservoir / bores** — town water supply
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Pipeline from Port Augusta** — connected to Morgan–Whyalla system
- **Notes:** Southern gateway to the Flinders Ranges. Pichi Richi Creek flows only after rain; reservoir and bores provide town water. The Pichi Richi Railway heritage steam train uses water from local sources.
- **Coordinates:** -32.3467, 138.0417

### Hawker
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Local creeks / bores** — town water supply; ephemeral surface water
- **Nearest reliable water:** 🟢 **Wilpena Creek / Wilpena Pound** — ~55 km north-east (permanent waterholes)
- **Notes:** Central Flinders Ranges; hub for the region. Town water from bores. Nearest permanent surface water is in the Wilpena Pound area. Local creeks (e.g., Wonoka Creek) flow only after significant rain.
- **Coordinates:** -31.8881, 138.4215

### Leigh Creek
- **Nearest water**: ⚠️ **Local bore fields** — town water (former coal-mining town)
- **Nearest natural water:** 🔴 **Aroona Dam** — ~10 km south-east (town supply, but managed/treated)
- **Alternative:** ⚠️ **Lake Torrens** — salt pan ~40 km west; undrinkable
- **Notes:** Purpose-built coal mining town (mine closed 2015). Town water from Aroona Dam plus bores. The dam is a significant water storage asset in the northern Flinders but access may be controlled. Flinders Ranges creeks within 30 km: Brachina, Parachilna.
- **Coordinates:** -30.5938, 138.4008

---

## Kangaroo Island

### Kingscote
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Middle River Reservoir** — ~15 km west (primary KI water supply)
- **Alternative:** 🟢 **Groundwater bores** — town supplementary supply
- **Nearest natural water:** 🔴 **Nepean Bay (sea)** — adjacent to town; saltwater
- **Notes:** Largest town on Kangaroo Island. Middle River Reservoir is the island's main water storage. KI relies on rainwater, surface catchments, and groundwater — no connection to the mainland water grid.
- **Coordinates:** -35.6545, 137.6387

### Penneshaw
- **Nearest water**: 🟡 **Local dams / rainwater tanks / bores** — town water supply
- **Better option:** 🟢 **Middle River Reservoir** — ~30 km west (via pipeline)
- **Nearest natural water:** 🔴 **Backstairs Passage (sea)** — adjacent; saltwater
- **Notes:** Ferry terminal town on the eastern end of Kangaroo Island. No permanent surface freshwater in the immediate area. Town water from a combination of local catchment, bores, and pipeline from the west. Rainwater tanks are common residential supply.
- **Coordinates:** -35.7207, 137.9414

> **Notes:**
> - Coordinates are approximate centre of each town.
> - "Distance" is to the nearest accessible point of the water body, not the town centre.
> - Towns on the Murray River have year-round access; the Riverland/Murraylands corridor is SA's most water-secure region.
> - Outback towns (Coober Pedy, Roxby Downs, Oodnadatta, Marree) are entirely dependent on Great Artesian Basin bores — if the bore infrastructure fails, there is no surface water backup.
> - Eyre Peninsula towns (Whyalla, Port Lincoln, Ceduna) rely on pipelines, bores, and desalination — natural freshwater is scarce.
> - Kangaroo Island has no mainland water connection — wholly dependent on local catchment and bores.
