# Water Sources — Northern NSW (Newcastle to Queensland Border)

> Source: OpenStreetMap
> Keywords: Newcastle water, Hunter Valley water, Port Stephens water, Taree water, Coffs Harbour water, Lismore water, Ballina water, Byron Bay water, Grafton water, Northern Rivers water sources

## Newcastle
- **Nearest water**: Hunter River (~5 km west of CBD). Pacific Ocean adjacent. Lake Macquarie (~15 km south).
- **Freshwater**: Chichester Dam area (Williams River, ~40 km north). Glenbawn Dam area (Hunter River upstream, ~100 km north-west).
- **Coordinates**: -32.928, 151.774

## Maitland
- **Nearest water**: Hunter River — runs through the city. Tidal, prone to flooding.
- **Coordinates**: -32.732, 151.558

## Port Stephens
- **Nearest water**: Port Stephens (large tidal estuary)
- **Nearest river**: Karuah River (~10 km north), Myall River (~15 km north)
- **Coordinates**: -32.700, 152.100

## Lake Macquarie
- **Nearest water**: Lake Macquarie — Australia's largest coastal saltwater lake, adjacent.
- **Alternative**: Dora Creek (main inflow, ~5 km west)
- **Coordinates**: -33.050, 151.600

## Taree
- **Nearest water**: Manning River — runs through the town. Tidal in the lower reaches.
- **Coordinates**: -31.900, 152.460

## Kempsey
- **Nearest water**: Macleay River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -31.080, 152.834

## Port Macquarie
- **Nearest water**: Hastings River (~3 km north-west of CBD). Pacific Ocean adjacent.
- **Coordinates**: -31.433, 152.908

## Coffs Harbour
- **Nearest water**: Coffs Harbour (ocean), Orara River (~10 km west, Clarence tributary)
- **Alternative**: Korora Basin, Moonee Beach
- **Coordinates**: -30.298, 153.116

## Grafton
- **Nearest water**: Clarence River — runs through the city. Largest coastal river in NSW by volume.
- **Coordinates**: -29.691, 152.934

## Lismore
- **Nearest water**: Wilsons River (Richmond River tributary) — runs through the city. Extremely prone to flooding (2022: largest recorded flood).
- **Coordinates**: -28.810, 153.287

## Ballina
- **Nearest water**: Richmond River — runs through the town. Pacific Ocean adjacent.
- **Coordinates**: -28.868, 153.568

## Byron Bay
- **Nearest water**: Pacific Ocean — adjacent. Brunswick River (~5 km north).
- **Coordinates**: -28.647, 153.603

## Murwillumbah
- **Nearest water**: Tweed River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -28.327, 153.394

## Casino
- **Nearest water**: Richmond River — runs through the town.
- **Coordinates**: -28.858, 153.047

## Moree
- **Nearest water**: Gwydir River (~3 km north). Major artesian basin — Moree is famous for artesian hot springs (Great Artesian Basin).
- **Coordinates**: -29.464, 149.849

## Inverell
- **Nearest water**: Macintyre River — runs through the town. Copeton Dam (~25 km south-east, Gwydir River).
- **Coordinates**: -29.774, 151.113

## Glen Innes
- **Nearest water**: Beardy River (Macintyre/Murray-Darling headwaters). Mann River (~15 km east, Clarence coastal system).
- **Coordinates**: -29.744, 151.737
