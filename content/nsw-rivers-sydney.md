## Hawkesbury-Nepean River System

The Hawkesbury-Nepean is the largest river system in the Sydney region, draining the entire Sydney Basin. Length: approximately 470 km from the headwaters of the Nepean to the Hawkesbury's mouth at Broken Bay.

The system flows as the **Nepean River** from its source in the Budawang Range, through the Southern Highlands (Goulburn, Camden), then becomes the **Hawkesbury River** at the confluence with the Grose River near Penrith. From there it winds through the Hawkesbury region to Broken Bay between Palm Beach and Barrenjoey.

### Major Tributaries
- **Grose River** — flows from the Blue Mountains (Mt Tomah, Blackheath area)
- **Warragamba River** — carries water from Warragamba Dam, major Sydney water supply
- **Coxs River** — flows from the Blue Mountains plateau through Lithgow area
- **Wollondilly River** — flows from the Southern Highlands, joins the Warragamba
- **Kowmung River** — wilderness river in the Blue Mountains, joins the Coxs
- **Colo River** — last major tributary before the sea, joins at Lower Portland
- **Macdonald River** — flows through St Albans area
- **Webbs Creek** — tributary near Wisemans Ferry
- **Mangrove Creek** — tributary near Central Coast

### Water Supply Dams on the System
- **Warragamba Dam** — Sydney's primary water supply (~80% of Sydney's water)
- **Nepean Dam** — southern Sydney supply
- **Avon Dam** — southern Sydney supply
- **Cataract Dam** — southern Sydney supply
- **Cordeaux Dam** — southern Sydney supply
- **Woronora Dam** — southern Sydney supply
- **Tallowa Dam** — Shoalhaven system, pumps water into the Nepean via the Shoalhaven Transfers Scheme

## Parramatta River

The Parramatta River forms the main tidal estuary of Sydney Harbour. Formed by the confluence of:
- **Lane Cove River** — flows from the Thornleigh/Cherrybrook area south through Lane Cove National Park
- **Duck River** — flows through Silverwater, Auburn
- **Haslams Creek** — flows through Homebush Bay

## Nearest Water Sources to Cherrybrook (33.73°S, 151.05°E)

Cherrybrook is in Sydney's Hills District, within the Hornsby Shire. The nearest natural water sources are:

**1. Lane Cove River** — 2-3 km east of Cherrybrook
- Access: Walk east through Thornleigh Oval, follow the Lane Cove River walking track
- Head east down Purchase Rd, turn into Banana St underpass to Thornleigh, follow the creek line
- The Lane Cove River starts near Thornleigh and flows south through Lane Cove National Park
- Coordinates: approximately -33.763, 151.104 (near Thornleigh)

**2. Berowra Creek** — 6-8 km north-east of Cherrybrook
- A major tributary of the Hawkesbury River
- Access via Berowra Valley National Park (Great North Walk)
- Deep, tidal water in its lower reaches
- Coordinates: approximately -33.689, 151.078 (near Crosslands Reserve)

**3. Cowan Creek** — 10-12 km north-east of Cherrybrook
- Tidal estuary of the Hawkesbury, near Brooklyn
- Access via Cowan Creek area (Kuring-gai Chase National Park)

**4. Hawkesbury River** — 15-20 km north of Cherrybrook
- Access via Wisemans Ferry or the Brooklyn bridge area
- Large tidal river, year-round water available
- Coordinates: approximately -33.520, 151.118 (Wisemans Ferry area)

**5. Pennant Hills Creek / Devils Creek** — 1-2 km south of Cherrybrook
- Small creek system flowing through Pennant Hills Park
- Seasonal flow only — may be dry in summer

## Central Coast Rivers

Between Sydney and Newcastle:
- **Wyong River** — flows through Wyong to Tuggerah Lake
- **Cedar Brush Creek** — tributary of Wyong River
- **Ourimbah Creek** — flows to Tuggerah Lake
- **Erina River** — flows to Avoca Lake
- **Karuah River** — flows through Bulahdelah to Port Stephens

## Hunter River System

The Hunter River (470 km) is the major river of the Hunter Region, flowing from the Liverpool Range to Newcastle.

### Tributaries (from mouth to source)
- **Paterson River** — flows through Paterson, joins near Maitland
- **Williams River** — flows through Dungog, water supply for Newcastle
- **Allyn River** — tributary of Paterson
- **Chichester River** — tributary of Williams, dammed for Newcastle supply
- **Goulburn River** — flows from the Merriwa plateau through the Goulburn River National Park
- **Wollombi Brook** — flows through Wollombi, wine country
- **Pages River** — flows from Murrurundi area
- **Isis River** — flows from the Liverpool Range

### Hunter Water Supply Dams
- **Chichester Dam** — on the Chichester River, primary Newcastle water supply
- **Grahamstown Dam** — large off-river storage near Raymond Terrace
- **Glennies Creek Dam** — on Glennies Creek, irrigation and coal mining
- **Lostock Dam** — on the Paterson River, irrigation
- **Lake Liddell** — cooling water for power stations

### Lake Macquarie (Central Coast)
- Largest coastal saltwater lake in NSW (110 km²)
- Connected to the Tasman Sea at Swansea
- Dora Creek is the main freshwater inflow
- Bull sharks present
