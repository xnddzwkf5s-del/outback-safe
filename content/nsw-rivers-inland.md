# Inland NSW Rivers — Murray-Darling Basin

> Source: Wikipedia, Murray-Darling Basin Authority
> Keywords: Murray River, Murrumbidgee River, Lachlan River, Darling River, Macquarie River, Namoi River, Gwydir River, inland rivers NSW, western NSW rivers, Murray-Darling Basin

## Overview

The Murray-Darling Basin covers ~1.06 million km², one-seventh of Australia's land area. It includes six of Australia's seven longest rivers. The basin drains west from the Great Dividing Range and flows south-west into the Great Australian Bight via the Murray River in South Australia.

## Murray River

- **Length**: 2,508 km — Australia's longest river
- **Source**: Snowy Mountains, near Mt Kosciuszko
- **Mouth**: Murray Mouth near Goolwa, South Australia
- **Tributaries (NSW side)**: Murrumbidgee River, Lachlan River, Darling River, Edward River, Wakool River, Niemur River
- **Key towns (NSW)**: Albury-Wodonga, Deniliquin, Moama-Echuca, Wentworth
- **Features**: The Murray forms the NSW/Victoria border for most of its length. Major weirs and locks (16 in total) regulate flow for irrigation. The Murray supports a $3 billion/year irrigation industry. Key reservoirs: **Dartmouth Dam** (headwaters), **Hume Dam** (main storage), **Lake Victoria** (regulatory storage).
- **Water quality**: Variable salinity, blue-green algae outbreaks common in summer
- **Navigation**: Paddle steamers operated historically. Limited navigation now due to weirs.
- **Fish**: Murray cod, golden perch, silver perch, trout cod (endangered)

## Murrumbidgee River

- **Length**: 1,485 km — second longest river in NSW
- **Source**: Snowy Mountains, near Kiandra
- **Mouth**: Joins the Murray near Boundary Bend
- **Key tributaries**: Molonglo River (Canberra), Cotter River, Yass River, Goodradigbee River, Tumut River, Lachlan River (through anabranch), Gogeldrie Creek
- **Key towns**: Canberra (via Molonglo), Yass, Gundagai, Wagga Wagga, Narrandera, Hay, Balranald
- **Features**: The river flows through the ACT. **Burrinjuck Dam** (near Yass) regulates the upper Murrumbidgee for irrigation. The **Snowy Mountains Scheme** contributes additional flow via the Eucumbene-Tumut tunnels. The river passes through extensive floodplain red gum forests. Wagga Wagga is the largest inland city on the river.
- **Flooding**: Gundagai is notorious for catastrophic floods (1852: 89 deaths, worst inland flood in Australian history)

## Darling River

- **Length**: 1,472 km — Australia's third longest river
- **Source**: Junction of the Barwon and Culgoa rivers near Bourke
- **Mouth**: Joins the Murray at Wentworth
- **Key tributaries**: Barwon River, Culgoa River, Bogan River, Paroo River, Warrego River
- **Key towns**: Bourke, Brewarrina, Louth, Tilpa, Wilcannia, Menindee, Wentworth
- **Features**: The Darling flows through semi-arid and arid country. Extremely variable flow — can be reduced to a chain of ponds during drought. In devastating floods it can reach 40 km wide across the floodplain. **Menindee Lakes** regulate flows. The Darling has experienced mass fish kills during drought (2018-19: hundreds of thousands of fish). The river is culturally significant — the Baakindji people have lived along it for millennia. Brewarrina Fish Traps (Ngunnhu) are among the oldest human structures in Australia (~40,000 years).
- **Wetland**: The lower Darling is part of the Murray-Darling's most significant floodplain ecosystem.

## Lachlan River

- **Length**: 1,339 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Gunning
- **Mouth**: Murrumbidgee River (through the Great Cumbung Swamp)
- **Key tributaries**: Abercrombie River, Belubula River, Boorowa River
- **Key towns**: Cowra, Forbes, Condobolin, Hillston, Booligal
- **Features**: The Lachlan ends in **Great Cumbung Swamp** (one of NSW's largest wetlands, ~500 km²). Extremely variable flow — zero surface flow reaches the Murrumbidgee in dry years. **Wyangala Dam** is the main storage. The river is known for its wetlands, birdlife, and as a prime Murray cod fishery.

## Macquarie River

- **Length**: 960 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Oberon
- **Mouth**: Macquarie Marshes (terminates)
- **Key tributaries**: Cudgegong River, Turon River, Bell River, Talbragar River, Wambangalong Creek
- **Key towns**: Bathurst, Wellington, Dubbo, Narromine, Warren
- **Features**: The Macquarie flows into the **Macquarie Marshes**, one of Australia's largest semi-permanent wetlands (~200,000 ha, an internationally significant Ramsar-listed wetland). **Burrendong Dam** is the main storage. The marshes are critical habitat for waterbirds. The river has high cultural significance for the Wiradjuri people. Bathurst was the earliest inland European settlement (1815).

## Namoi River

- **Length**: 540 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range, Niangala
- **Mouth**: Barwon River near Walgett
- **Key tributaries**: Peel River (Tamworth), Manilla River, Mooki River, Coxs Creek
- **Key towns**: Tamworth (via Peel River), Gunnedah, Narrabri, Wee Waa
- **Features**: Significant for irrigation (cotton, grain). **Keepit Dam** and **Split Rock Dam** regulate flows. The Namoi Valley is some of the richest agricultural land in NSW. Cotton production is a major industry. The river supports a diverse fish population in its upper reaches.

## Gwydir River

- **Length**: 490 km
- **Source**: Great Dividing Range near Glen Innes
- **Mouth**: Barwon River near Collarenebri
- **Key tributaries**: Horton River, Rocky River
- **Key towns**: Bingara, Moree
- **Features**: The Gwydir Wetlands (Ramsar-listed) are a significant environmental feature. **Copeton Dam** provides irrigation storage. Moree is a major artesian spring town (Great Artesian Basin). The Gwydir Valley produces cotton, almonds, and grain.

## Castlereagh River

- **Length**: 480 km
- **Source**: Warrumbungle Range
- **Mouth**: Macquarie River
- **Key towns**: Mendooran, Gilgandra, Coonamble
- **Features**: The Castlereagh has no major dams — highly unregulated. Flows through the Warrumbungle National Park. Intermittent — can be dry for extended periods.

## Barwon River

- **Length**: 700 km
- **Source**: Junction of the Macintyre River and Weir River near Mungindi
- **Mouth**: Becomes the Darling River at the junction with the Culgoa near Bourke
- **Key tributaries**: Macintyre River, Weir River, Boomi River, Gwydir River, Namoi River, Castlereagh River, Mooni River
- **Key towns**: Mungindi, Collarenebri, Walgett, Brewarrina
- **Features**: The Barwon forms part of the Darling River system. Brewarrina Fish Traps (Ngunnhu) are a major cultural site — ancient Aboriginal fish traps. Irrigation along the Barwon supports cotton and grain.

## Culgoa River

- **Length**: 340 km
- **Source**: Balonne River, Queensland
- **Mouth**: Joins Barwon to form the Darling
- **Features**: The Culgoa River flows through the Culgoa National Park. Floodplain wetlands support significant birdlife. Low and intermittent in dry years.

## Other Western Rivers

### Paroo River
- **Length**: 475 km
- **Source**: Queensland, near Charleville
- **Mouth**: Paroo Overflow — terminates in a series of lakes including Lake Peery, Lake Bulloo
- **Features**: The Paroo is one of the last unregulated river systems in the Murray-Darling Basin. **No major dams**. The Paroo Overflow Lakes are Ramsar-listed wetlands. The Paroo River is the most intact arid-zone river system in Australia.

### Warrego River
- **Length**: 1,380 km
- **Source**: Queensland, near Augathella
- **Mouth**: Darling River near Bourke
- **Features**: Highly intermittent — no water for years at a time in the lower reaches. **Cunnamulla** in Queensland is the main town. The river has significant floodplain ecosystems in the lower reaches.
