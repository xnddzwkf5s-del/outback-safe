## Sydney Region — Dural

The Dural 2M repeater (VK2RWI) is located at Dural, NSW and operates on 147.000 MHz. Dural coverage extends across Sydney.

- **VK2RWI Dural: 147.000 MHz (input 146.400), CTCSS 91.5 Hz, 120W ERP, 240m HASL**
- Amateur licence required. Standard -600 kHz offset.
- Source: WIA Repeater Directory, September 2025
